Disk safeguards

Before and after erasure.

Before erasure

  • Only whole USB-attached or explicitly removable disks.
  • Fixed, system, read-only, undersized, and partition targets rejected.
  • Source, target, operations, and effects shown before confirmation.
  • Stable ID rechecked and child volumes unmounted before writing.

After writing

  • Exact written range read back and compared with SHA-256.
  • Compressed sources compared after expansion.
  • Windows boot files, payloads, split-WIM parts, and FAT32 limits audited.
  • Failed or cancelled media marked incomplete; full rewrite required.

Scope: verification covers bytes or boot structure, not installation success.
